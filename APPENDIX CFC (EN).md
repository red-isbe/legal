**APPENDIX – GENERAL FINANCIAL CONTRIBUTIONS**

*Framework Agreement for Membership of the ISBE Network*

**Effective: 1 July 2026 · Validity: 12 months (until 30 June 2027\) · Amounts in EUR, VAT not included**

# **1\. Scope and validity**

This tariff Appendix sets out the financial terms referred to by the Framework Agreement for Membership of the ISBE Network (Clause Three, section 4 – General financial contributions) and by Policy ADM1010 – Policy on Fees and Remuneration. Both documents are independent of, and more static than, this Appendix.

Amounts are expressed in euros and do not include VAT. Fees are fixed; there are no gas fees or variable per-transaction costs, except for the Network Service Provider Programme overage set out in section 3.3.

This tariff Appendix takes effect on 1 July 2026 and shall remain valid for twelve (12) months, until 30 June 2027\. Upon expiry, and until a subsequent version is published, the latest version in force shall remain applicable.

The Qualified Electronic Ledger (QEL) service on the Bare Network is not included in this tariff Appendix and is governed by a specific instrument.
