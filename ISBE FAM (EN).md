**DECLARE** 

I. That ISBE is a national permissioned public blockchain infrastructure, whose explicit  governance and tiered participation system ensure a balance between the openness of  the ecosystem and the requirements of security, resilience and legal compliance. 

II. That the ISBE network operates under a public-permissioned model, which means that, whilst it is an open and collaborative infrastructure, access to certain functionalities is  restricted to authorised participants according to their participation level (the  “**Participation Level**”). The ecosystem is structured into the following Participation  Levels, each of which is set out in the relevant annex to this Framework Agreement: a. Basic; 

b. Observer; 

c. Developer;  
d. Execution Node Operator; and 

e. Consensus Node Operator. 

III. Each of the aforementioned Participation Levels has its own set of rights, operational  capabilities and obligations within the ISBE network. The Participation Levels are  independent of one another, with no hierarchy or mandatory progression between  them; a Adhering Entity may subscribe to one or more Participation Levels  simultaneously, provided that it fully complies with the obligations associated with each  and respects any required functional segregation, particularly between technical and  infrastructure roles. Multiple membership is voluntary and, under no circumstances,  mandatory. 

IV. That, in order to preserve the neutrality, security and federated nature of the ISBE  network, the accumulation of Participation Levels that may compromise these  principles may be subject to conditions, limited or, where appropriate, revoked by the  competent supervisory or governance body of the Managing Entity (as defined below),  by means of a reasoned decision, based on objective, transparent and non discriminatory criteria and in accordance with the ISBE Policies, this power acting as  a mechanism to prevent concentrations of power or infrastructure capture. 

V. That the legal relationship between the Adhering Entity and the Managing Entity is  structured through the following contractual framework, with the Managing Entity  acting as the sole contractual counterparty to the Adhering Entity and as the body  responsible for the administration and coordination of the ISBE network, in accordance  with the decisions of its governance bodies: 

• By signing this Framework Agreement for membership of the ISBE network, which  establishes the general legal framework, the mandatory ISBE Policies and the  common liability regime applicable to all members of the network. 

• By signing, as an integral part thereof, at least one of the participation level  annexes (the “**Role Annexes**”), each of which governs the specific regime  applicable to the corresponding Participation Level. 

The Adhering Entity’s accession to a Role Annex shall take place: *(I)* automatically,  in the case of the Basic and Developer Levels, once the application has been  formalised through the channels enabled by the Managing Entity and the minimum  checks set out in the ISBE Policies have been verified, without the need for an  individual review process; and *(II)* for all other Participation Levels, only once the  requirements applicable to the relevant Participation Level have been met and  following review and approval by the Managing Entity, in accordance with the  application procedures established by ISBE’s governance. 

Membership of ISBE is not permitted solely through the signing of the Framework  Agreement without the simultaneous assumption of at least one Participation Level.

**PROVISIONS** 

**FIRST.— PURPOSE** 

1\. The purpose of this Framework Agreement is to establish the general legal framework for  membership and participation in the ISBE network and the legal relationship between the  Adhering Entity and the Managing Entity, a framework which applies to any organisation  holding the status of Participant, regardless of the Participation Level(s) it assumes. 

2\. The specific rights, operational capacities, limitations and obligations of each Participation  Level are set out and specified in the corresponding Role Annexes which, where applicable,  the Adhering Entity shall sign in its capacity as a Participant, and which form an integral part  of this Framework Agreement and supplement it. 

3\. The signing of this Framework Agreement alone does not confer on the Adhering Entity the  status of Participant in the ISBE network. To acquire such status, it will be necessary, in all  cases, to simultaneously sign the Framework Agreement and at least one Role Annex  relating to one of the available Participation Levels, at which point the Adhering Entity shall  be subject both to the general provisions of this Framework Agreement and to the specific  provisions of the signed Role Annex(es). 

4\. The subsequent adoption of new Participation Levels by the Adhering Entity shall require  the signing of the corresponding additional Role Annexes and prior verification by the  Managing Entity of compliance with the applicable requirements in each case, in accordance  with the ISBE Policies. The termination of a specific Role Annex shall not affect, unless  expressly agreed otherwise or unless it is the sole Role Annex, the validity of this Framework  Agreement or the remaining Levels of Participation that the Adhering Entity maintains in  force. 

**SECOND.— DEFINITIONS** 

For the purposes of this Framework Agreement, the following terms shall have the meanings  set out below: 

• “**ISBE**”: Acronym for *Infraestructura de Servicios Blockchain de España* (Spanish  Blockchain Services Infrastructure), a public-permissioned blockchain network promoted  by public administrations and private entities, aligned with European guidelines on  interoperability and digital trust. It comprises a set of networks (base network and Use  Case networks, amongst others) designed to support applications with various levels of  openness and regulatory requirements.  
• “**Managing Entity**”: The entity responsible for the operational, technical and governance  management of the ISBE network, authorised to enter into this Framework Agreement  on behalf of the network. It exercises the relevant administrative faculties, including the  admission of participants, monitoring compliance with policies and the eventual exclusion  of participants for non-compliance. 

• “**ISBE Policies**”: A set of internal policies governing various aspects of the ISBE network  and its ecosystem, approved by the Managing Body in the exercise of its governance  functions. Some of these policies apply internally to the Managing Entity and the  governing bodies of ISBE, whilst others are mandatory for Adhering Entities in general or  for specific Adhering Entities depending on their Participation Level or Levels. The current  version of the ISBE Policies and their scope of application shall be kept up to date and  made available via the ISBE portal or other channels designated for this purpose, without  prejudice to the specific indication of applicable policies set out in the tables included in  each Role Annex. The procedure for the approval, amendment and precedence of the  ISBE Policies over this Framework Agreement shall be as provided for in Clause Seven.  The ISBE Policies which, according to the information in force at any given time, are  applicable to the Adhering Entity shall be deemed incorporated into this Framework  Agreement by reference and shall be binding upon the Adhering Entity within the scope  of its participation in ISBE. 

• “**Participant**”: A legal entity that has signed this Framework Agreement and,  simultaneously or successively, one or more Role Annexes relating to the envisaged  Participation Levels, having passed the *Know Your Business* (KYB) verification process  and met the other requirements set out in the ISBE Policies. 

• “**Basic**”: A Participation Level that grants the Adhering Entity a set of limited and non intrusive operational capabilities on the ISBE network, enabling it, under the terms  specified in the ISBE Policies: **(i)** to access the private area and the ISBE portal; **(ii)** to  use pre-configured smart contract libraries and wizards in non-programmatic and  supervised deployment modes; and **(iii)** to use the basic services that ISBE makes  available to its Participants (including, where applicable, notarisation and evidence  registration services); **(iv)** to use, where applicable, the controlled gas acquisition  mechanisms that ISBE makes available to this Participation Level (faucet or other  equivalents); and **(v)** receive administrative communications and functional support via  the ISBE private area and the designated HelpDesk channels, without being authorised  to operate its own nodes or to interact directly with the blockchain beyond the flows  enabled and supervised by ISBE. The Adhering Entity may assume this Participation  Level once it has **(i)** signed **Annex I – Specific Conditions of the Basic Level** and **(ii)**  successfully completed the general internal admission procedure set out in **Policy  ECO1010 – Policy on joining and leaving the ISBE ecosystem** and other applicable  ISBE Policies. 

• “**Observer**”: A Participation Level aimed at Participating Entities with supervisory, control,  audit, research or institutional interest functions, which allows them to audit and monitor  the ISBE network without the ability to intervene operationally in it or issue transactions.  This access comprises read-only exploration of the state of the ISBE network (blocks,  transactions, metrics and aggregated states), as well as the use of advanced  observability tools made available by the Managing Entity (monitoring dashboards,  scorecards and operational logs), which allow the Observer to analyse the system’s   
behaviour, its stability and the temporal evolution of its main indicators, without the power  to issue transactions or modify the network status, and in accordance with the terms and  scope set out in the ISBE Policies. The Adhering Entity may assume this Participation  Level once it has ***(i)*** signed **Annex II — Specific Conditions of the Observer Level** and  ***(ii)*** successfully passed the internal assessment and suitability procedure applicable to  this Participation Level, in accordance with **Policy ECO1010 – Policy on joining and  leaving the ISBE ecosystem** and the other ISBE Policies. 

• “**Developer**”: A Participation Level that enables the Adhering Entity to design, develop,  deploy and maintain Use Cases and Smart Contracts on the ISBE networks, subject to  passing the technical and regulatory approval and compliance processes set out in the  ISBE Policies. At this Participation Level, the Adhering Entity may, in addition to the  functional capabilities associated with the Basic Level where compatible: ***(i)*** deploy its  own Smart Contracts that have passed the approval tests set out in **Policy ECO1020 – Policy on the Management of Use Cases and SCs**; ***(ii)*** use the *Blockchain-as-a* 

*Service* infrastructure services that ISBE makes available to Developers or which, where  applicable, are provided to them by third-party Execution Node Operators; and ***(iii)***  request the inclusion of their solutions in the ISBE catalogue of services and applications,  subject to the terms and limits defined in the ISBE Policies. The Developer shall not be  authorised to operate its own Execution Nodes or to expose node infrastructure under its  control on the ISBE network, unless the Adhering Entity has additionally assumed the  Execution Node Operator Level. The Adhering Entity may assume this Participation Level  when it has: ***(i)*** signed **Annex III — Specific Conditions of the Developer Participation  Level** and ***(ii)*** successfully passed the internal technical approval and compliance  procedure set out for this Level in **Policy ECO1020 – Policy on the Management of  Use Cases and SCs** and other applicable ISBE Policies. 

• “**Execution Node Operator**”: A Participation Level that enables the Adhering Entity to  deploy, configure and operate one or more of its own Execution Nodes within the ISBE  network (as distinct from Consensus Nodes), thereby assuming an infrastructure role  within the ecosystem. At this Participation Level, the Adhering Entity may, subject to the  terms set out in the ISBE Policies***: (i)*** use its Execution Nodes as a transactional access  point to the ISBE network for its own systems and applications (for example, via RPC  endpoints, APIs or specific connectors); ***(ii)*** provide, where permitted, connectivity and  *Blockchain-as-a-Service* to authorised third parties, relying on said Execution Nodes;  ***(iii)*** integrate the Execution Nodes into its own systems for monitoring, event logging,  observability and incident management; and ***(iv)*** participate in the high-availability,  redundancy and resilience mechanisms defined for the execution layer of the ISBE  network, without intervening in the Consensus Protocol. An Adhering Entity holding this  Participation Level must comply with the technical, security, provisioning, availability,  capacity and support requirements defined for Execution Nodes in **Policy INF1040 – Node Management Policy** and in the other ISBE Policies, including, where applicable,  the obligation to maintain certain service levels (SLAs), keep technical records and  cooperate in audits or verifications carried out on the infrastructure. The Adhering Entity may assume this Participation Level once it has: ***(i)*** signed **Annex IV — Specific  Conditions for the Execution Node Operator Level** and ***(ii)*** successfully passed the  internal technical and node management certification procedure applicable to this Level,  in accordance with **Policy INF1040 – Node Management Policy** and the other ISBE  Policies.  
• “**Consensus Node Operator**”: A Participation Level that enables the Adhering Entity to  operate one or more Consensus Nodes and actively participate in the ISBE network’s  Consensus Protocol, contributing to the validation of transactions and to the security and  integrity of the infrastructure. At this Participation Level, the Adhering Entity assumes  enhanced obligations regarding independence, location, security, availability and  compliance with the Consensus Protocol parameters defined in the ISBE Policies. The  Consensus Node must be deployed at an identified, stable physical location previously  notified to the Managing Entity, situated within the territory of a Member State of the  European Union or the European Economic Area or in any of the territories authorised in  **Policy INF1040 – Node Management Policy**, and must at all times comply with the  technical, security, availability and independence conditions set out in the ISBE Policies.  The Adhering Entity may assume this Participation Level when it has: ***(I)*** signed **Annex V  — Specific Conditions for the Consensus Node Operator Level**; and ***(II)*** successfully  passed the internal technical, security and governance approval procedure provided for  this Level, in accordance with **Policy INF1040 – Node Management Policy** and the other  ISBE Policies, including any restrictions on the accumulation of roles and segregation of  duties that may be provided for this Level of Participation. 

• “**Consensus Protocol**”: A set of rules, algorithms and technical parameters that  determine how transaction blocks are proposed, validated and finalised on the ISBE  network, through the exclusive intervention of Consensus Nodes operated by entities  holding the Consensus Node Operator Level and which have been previously authorised  in accordance with this Framework Agreement and the ISBE Policies. It includes,  amongst other elements, the criteria for the selection and rotation of Consensus Nodes,  the quorum and voting rules, the thresholds required for block validation, the conditions  for transaction finality, and the procedures for managing incidents and failures in the  consensus. The definition, review, updating and, where applicable, replacement of the  Consensus Protocol is a power reserved for the Management Entity, in accordance with  the decisions of the ISBE governance bodies and the ISBE Policies, and shall be carried  out exclusively through the corresponding software modifications, network configurations  and/or governance smart contracts. No Participant may unilaterally alter or attempt to  alter the Consensus Protocol, nor promote forks or unauthorised modifications thereof. 

• “**Execution Node**”: A node on the ISBE network responsible for processing transactions,  maintaining the state of the blockchain and providing services for reading and, where  applicable, issuing transactions, without directly intervening in the Consensus Protocol.  It may be operated by the Management Entity or by entities holding the Execution Node  Operator Level, in accordance with **Policy INF1040 – Node Management Policy** and  the other ISBE Policies. 

• “**Consensus Node**”: A node that forms part of the ISBE network’s Consensus Protocol,  responsible for proposing and validating transaction blocks. Its operation is subject to  strict technical, security (hardening) and availability requirements defined in **Policy  INF1040 – Node Management Policy**. 

• “**Use Case**”: A technological solution (application, service or business process) that  utilises the ISBE infrastructure to execute business logic, record transactions and/or  anchor evidence, typically through one or more Smart Contracts deployed on ISBE  networks other than the base network. Each Use Case must be previously authorised  and approved in accordance with **Policy ECO1020 – Policy on the Management of**   
**Use Cases and SCs**, and may, at the request of the Adhering Entity holding the  Developer or Execution Node Operator Level, be included in the ISBE service catalogue  once deployed. 

• “**Smart Contract**”: A program or set of codes deployed on the ISBE network that  automatically executes predefined conditions. Its deployment on the ISBE network  requires passing a technical and cybersecurity compliance test carried out by the  Managing Entity, and must be carried out using authorised factory contracts to ensure  traceability and compliance with the approved architecture (the “**Factory Contracts**”). 

• “**DORA Use Case**”: A Use Case deployed or to be deployed on the ISBE network which:  *(I)* forms part of the ICT services used by one or more financial institutions falling within  the scope of Regulation (EU) 2022/2554 (“**DORA Regulation**”) for the provision of their  regulated services; or *(II)* is used by the Adhering Entity under a contract with a financial  institution subject to the DORA Regulation in which it is contractually required to ensure  certain digital operational resilience requirements regarding the infrastructure used,  including ISBE. 

• “**DORA Addendum**”: A contractual document supplementing the Framework Agreement  that incorporates the specific clauses required by the DORA Regulation and/or by the  financial institutions subject to it, in relation to the ICT Services described in the  Addendum itself (and its annex of services), which may correspond to one or more Use  Cases deployed on the ISBE network. The DORA Addendum shall identify the DORA  Use Cases covered, the role of the Adhering Entity in said DORA Use Cases, and the  scope of the additional commitments undertaken by the Managing Entity for the sole  purpose of facilitating compliance with the DORA Regulation by the financial institutions  concerned and/or by the Adhering Entity itself. 

• “**DORA Entity**”: Any entity falling within the subjective scope of application of the DORA  Regulation, whether the Adhering Entity itself or a client of the Adhering Entity in respect  of whom a DORA Use Case is deployed on the ISBE network.

**SEVENTH.— TERM, AMENDMENT AND TERMINATION OF THE FRAMEWORK  AGREEMENT**

This Framework Agreement shall be effective from the date of its signature by both Parties  and shall have an initial term of **one (1) year** from that date, remaining in force for as long  as the Adhering Entity has signed at least one Role Annex relating to a Participation Level  in the ISBE network. 

Upon expiry of this initial period, the Framework Agreement shall be tacitly extended for  successive periods of one (1) year, unless either Party notifies the other in writing of its  intention not to extend it, giving at least three (3) months’ notice prior to the expiry date of  the initial period or any of its extensions. 

Each Role Annex signed shall form an integral part of the Framework Agreement and its  validity shall be subject to that of the Framework Agreement, unless the relevant Role  Annex is terminated individually in accordance with its own clauses3. The termination of the  last Role Annex maintained in force by the Adhering Entity shall, in any event, result in the  automatic termination of this Framework Agreement on the same date on which such  termination takes effect.

**3\. Termination of the Framework Agreement** 

*1\. Voluntary withdrawal by the Adhering Entity* 

The Adhering Entity may, at any time, terminate its participation in the ISBE network and,  consequently, terminate this Framework Agreement, by giving written notice to the  Managing Entity at least fifteen (15) calendar days in advance, in accordance with the  voluntary withdrawal procedure set out in **Policy ECO1010 – Policy on joining and  leaving the ISBE ecosystem**.

**Annex V – Specific Conditions for the Consensus Node Operator Level 1\. Applicability** 

This annex shall apply where the Adhering Entity has signed the Framework Agreement  and has been authorised to hold the **Consensus Node Operator** Level within the ISBE  network, in accordance with **Policy ECO1010 – Policy on joining and leaving the ISBE  ecosystem** and **Policy INF1040 – Node Management Policy**. 

The possible combination of this Participation Level with other Participation Levels shall  only be permitted where provided for in the ISBE Policies and where the requirements  regarding functional segregation, independence and differentiation of equipment, procedures and infrastructure established by those policies **are met**. 

**2\. Purpose of the Annex** 

To establish the specific conditions under which the Adhering Entity shall perform the  Consensus Node Operator Level, considered critical to the security and operation of the  ISBE network due to its involvement in the Consensus Protocol and the validation of  transactions. 

This Annex shall supplement the Framework Agreement by referring, in relation to technical  commitments, service levels and, where applicable, financial compensation associated with  the operation of the Consensus Node, to **the provisions** of the applicable ISBE Policies, in  particular **Policy INF1040 – Node Management Policy** and **Policy ADM1010 – Policy on  Fees and Remuneration**. 

**3\. Accreditation as a Consensus Node Operator** 

In order for the Adhering Entity to qualify for the Consensus Node Operator Level, it must,  in all cases: 

a. meet the general requirements for admission **to** and continued membership of the ISBE  ecosystem as set out in **Policy ECO1010 – Policy on joining and leaving the ISBE  ecosystem**; 

b. demonstrate sufficient technical capability and resources to operate a Consensus Node  in accordance with the specifications of **Policy INF1040 – Node Management Policy**  (including, but not limited to, hardware, connectivity and security requirements); 

c. pass the technical, security and, where applicable, resilience tests provided for in **Policy  INF1040 – Node Management Policy** and in the other applicable ISBE Policies; and 

d. sign this annex, thereby formalising their commitment to operate the Consensus Node  diligently and to comply with the operational guidelines issued by the Managing Entity in  accordance with the ISBE Policies. 

The Management Entity, once it has assessed compliance with the above requirements  and depending on the needs of the ISBE network, may approve the inclusion of the  
Adhering Entity into the set of authorised Consensus Nodes, in accordance with the  provisions of **Policy INF1040 – Node Management Policy**. 

The Managing Entity shall provide the Adhering Entity with the configuration elements,  credentials and authorisations necessary for the Consensus Node to be recognised by the  ISBE network, in accordance with **Policy INF1050 – Policy on the management of digital  identities, credentials and access** and **Policy INF1040 – Node Management Policy**. 

The specific conditions for joining the set of Consensus Nodes (including, where applicable,  participation as an active or standby node and any applicable rotation schemes) shall be  governed entirely by **Policy INF1040 – Node Management Policy** and other applicable  ISBE Policies. 

**4\. Obligations of the Consensus Node Operator** 

*a. Operation of the Consensus Node*: The Adhering Entity undertakes to operate its  Consensus Node on a continuous basis and in accordance with the technical, hardware  and cybersecurity specifications established from time to time by **Policy INF1040 – Node  Management Policy**, maintaining a dedicated and appropriately certified operating  environment. 

*b. Availability and Performance*: The Adhering Entity shall maintain the levels of availability  and performance defined in **Policy INF1040 – Node Management Policy**, including the  service level agreements (SLAs) set out therein. To this end, it shall accept that the  Managing Entity monitors the Consensus Node and records the necessary metrics, in  accordance with **Policy INF1040 – Node Management Policy** and with the incident  management procedures established therein under its internal policies. 

*c. Active participation in the consensus*: The Adhering Entity shall act diligently in proposing  and signing blocks under the Consensus Protocol in force, without introducing deliberate  delays or unjustifiably rejecting the validation of transactions. Where maintenance tasks  or other actions that temporarily affect its participation are necessary, the Adhering Entity 

shall coordinate this with the Managing Entity in accordance with the provisions of **Policy  INF1040 – Node Management Policy**. 

*d. Client software integrity*: The Adhering Entity shall refrain from modifying, decompiling or  altering the ISBE client *software* deployed on the Consensus Node, its components,  libraries or modules, as well as from developing, installing or using *plugins*, extensions or  other mechanisms intended to manipulate, intercept or inspect transactions or the internal  operation of the node beyond the capabilities and tools expressly authorised by the  Managing Entity. Any update, version change or configuration adjustment to *the* client *software* shall be carried out exclusively in accordance with the deployment instructions  and procedures communicated by the Managing Entity. 

*e. Limitations and proper use*: The Adhering Entity shall use its Consensus Node exclusively  for participation in the ISBE network’s Consensus Protocol and, in particular:  
a. It shall refrain from using it to deploy smart contracts or execute business logic other  than that strictly necessary for said Consensus Protocol. 

b. It shall not use it to serve general network read traffic or to expose public access  interfaces, unless expressly provided for in **Policy INF1040 – Node Management  Policy**. 

c. It shall not connect it to corporate systems other than those strictly necessary for its  monitoring, management and secure operation, in accordance with the terms set out  in **Policy INF1040 – Node Management Policy**. 

The Consensus Node must operate in a closed-network environment, communicating  only with other authorised nodes on the ISBE network and with the Adhering Entity’s own  management and monitoring systems, as defined in **Policy INF1040 – Node  Management Policy**. 

The Consensus Node must remain deployed at the physical location and under the  infrastructure conditions declared by the Adhering Entity and accepted by the Managing  Entity, and must in all cases be located within one of the territories listed as authorised  in **Policy INF1040 – Node Management Policy**. Any material change to the location or  significant configuration of the Consensus Node shall comply with the notification,  validation and approval procedures set out in that policy. 

If the Adhering Entity wishes to temporarily or permanently cease the operation of its  Consensus Node, it shall notify the Managing Entity in accordance with the notice period  required by **Policy ECO1010 – Policy on joining and leaving the ISBE ecosystem**, so  that appropriate mitigation measures may be taken. 

*f. Compliance with INF1040*: In general, the Adhering Entity **shall comply** with all applicable  sections of **Policy INF1040 – Node Management Policy**, including updating the software  to the **required** versions, applying the recommended security configurations, responding  to monitoring alerts, and adhering to the procedures for deauthorisation and orderly  removal of the Consensus Node where applicable. 

*g. Collaboration with technical governance*: The Adhering Entity shall cooperate, to the  extent reasonably practicable, with the Management Entity and the technical governance  bodies in accordance with the terms set out in **Policy INF1040 – Node Management  Policy** and any other applicable ISBE Policies, providing accurate information about its  Consensus Node when requested for diagnostic, improvement or risk assessment  purposes, and implementing without undue delay any configuration changes or urgent  patches indicated for security reasons. 

**5\. Remuneration and costs** 

The financial terms associated with the Consensus Node Operator Level, including, where  applicable, any periodic payments that the Management Entity may grant to the Adhering Entity for the operation of its Consensus Node, shall be governed in full by the provisions  of **Policy ADM1010 – Policy on Fees and Remuneration**, in the version in force at any  given time.  
Any financial compensation shall be subject to compliance with the service levels and other  requirements set out in **Policy ADM1010 – Policy on Fees and Remuneration**, and failure  to comply may result in reductions or the withdrawal of compensation or, where applicable,  a review of the Participation Level itself in accordance with the Framework Agreement and  **Policy ECO1010 – Policy on joining and leaving the ISBE ecosystem**. 

All costs associated with the installation, operation and maintenance of the Consensus  Node (including, but not limited to, equipment, connectivity, energy, *cloud* services and  human resources) shall be borne in full by the Adhering Entity. Any compensation that may  be provided for in **Policy ADM1010 – Policy on Fees and Remuneration** shall be of a  purely contributory nature and shall not guarantee full coverage of such costs. 

Collaboration as a Consensus Node Operator shall be strictly of a civil or commercial nature  and shall not imply the existence of any employment relationship between the Adhering Entity and the Managing Entity or with the other participants, the Adhering Entity being free  to organise, under its sole responsibility, the technical and human resources it allocates to  the operation of the Consensus Node.

Furthermore, the financial consequences arising from failure to meet these service levels,  including the possible total or partial loss of compensation during the affected periods, **shall  be governed** by the provisions of **Policy ADM1010 – Policy on Fees and Remuneration**.
